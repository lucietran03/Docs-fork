How the project works

Each Squad consists of two teams. Each team develops a subsystem and integrates it with the other team’s subsystem, so collaboration and coordination are central to the assignment.

This semester also includes an "Ultra HD" requirement involving collaboration across Squads. Squads will develop a shared Property Management System (PMS), allowing available rooms in one ecosystem to be visible to users in another. This demonstrates coopetition: businesses cooperate on shared resources or technology while continuing to compete through their own services and strategies.
